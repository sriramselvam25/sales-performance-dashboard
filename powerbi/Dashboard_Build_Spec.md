# Power BI Dashboard Build Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
KPI cards: Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Gross Margin %, YoY Growth %, Target Attainment %, Average Order Value.

Visuals:
- Monthly Revenue vs Target trend
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Product Category
- Gross Margin % vs Revenue by Product
- Channel contribution

Slicers: Year, Region, Country, Product Category, Channel.

## Page 2 — Regional & Product Performance
- Region > Country matrix with Revenue, YoY %, Margin %, Target Attainment %
- Top / Bottom 10 Products
- Category contribution
- Discount % vs Gross Margin %
- Product drill-through

## Page 3 — Sales Team Performance
- Salesperson leaderboard
- Revenue vs Target by Salesperson
- Gross Margin % by Salesperson
- Team performance by Region
- Tooltip with rank, revenue, margin and attainment

## Page 4 — Trends & Drivers
- Monthly revenue seasonality
- Channel trend
- Discount impact
- Revenue variance waterfall
- Decomposition tree: Region > Country > Category > Product > Channel

## Formatting
- Canvas: 16:9
- Percentages: 0.0%
- Target conditional formatting: red <90%, amber 90–99.9%, green >=100%
- Limit each page to 5–7 primary visuals and preserve whitespace.
