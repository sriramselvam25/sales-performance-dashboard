# Illustrative Insights & Recommendations

All findings below are generated from the synthetic portfolio scenario and are intended to demonstrate analytical reasoning rather than represent any real company.

## Illustrative Findings
- The synthetic model is designed to produce positive 2025 YoY growth while still exposing pockets of underperformance.
- APAC carries the strongest regional weighting, enabling regional-contribution analysis and comparison against Europe, North America, MEA and Latin America.
- Q4 demand is intentionally stronger, supporting seasonality and capacity-planning analysis.
- Selected products carry intentionally higher cost ratios so the dashboard can identify high-revenue / weak-margin SKUs.
- Online orders have a higher typical discount profile, enabling analysis of the relationship between promotion intensity and margin.
- Latin America is assigned more challenging target conditions to demonstrate target-gap analysis.
- Salespeople are distributed across five regions so the report can support territory and coaching analysis.

## Recommendations
1. Prioritize below-target regions and channels with focused commercial action plans.
2. Review low-margin SKUs for pricing, promotion and cost-to-serve opportunities.
3. Protect Q4 stock and selling capacity ahead of seasonal demand peaks.
4. Use salesperson target attainment and margin together for coaching and territory planning.
5. Monitor average discount alongside gross margin rather than revenue alone.
6. Review category mix by region to identify localized growth opportunities.
